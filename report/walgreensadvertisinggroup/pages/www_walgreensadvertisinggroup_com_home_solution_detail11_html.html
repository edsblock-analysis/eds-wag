All offsite solutions

  • wagDSP: Programmatic Managed Service

    Proprietary wagID combined with powerful AI and omnichannel insights across 15,000 publishers. Fully managed service with dedicated team members.

  • DSP & SSP Self-Service

    A premium DSP and the most flexible Drug Store offering, wag has over 100 syndicated audience and custom audience capabilities with The Trade Desk; plus SSP options with OpenX.

  • CTV

    Reach your audience on premium streaming services with Infillion's static, video or interactive ad formats to drive immense engagement & reach. CTV options also available through wagDSP.

  • Social

    Engage and convert Walgreens audiences across Meta (Facebook & Instagram), Snap, and Pinterest, with a full suite of ad products. Brand influencer boosting available. Additionally, Healthcare advertisers can reach needed audiences through special social offerings.

  • Search

    Leverage wag's 1PD across Google's PMax, Shopping, Search platforms and reach consumers are in research mode and primed to purchase.
