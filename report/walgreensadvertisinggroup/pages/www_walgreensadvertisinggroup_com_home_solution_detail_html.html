Omnichannel opportunities

Walgreens has one of the largest retail footprints in the US, with ~75% of people living within 5 miles of a store. wag captures loyalty members’ in-store activities that tell us more than just what they purchased - it also reveals detailed insights as to what they might be open to buying, new products they may want to explore, and their brand loyalty level. Sync our wide-spectrum of in-store solutions to what your brand wants to achieve.

Branded Signage

Break the tie at the shelf: Engage your customer when they are actively making their purchasing decision. In-aisle signage, as well as signage throughout other key areas in the store, and more branded opportunities influence and incentivize shoppers at ~8,700 Walgreens locations.

Audio
