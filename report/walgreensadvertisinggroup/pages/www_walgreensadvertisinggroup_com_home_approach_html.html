Meet Maria

Maria’s habits are typical of millions of Walgreens shoppers – buyers focused on starting and maintaining healthy habits for their families. Here’s a close-up on how she shops.

  • Mom, 42
  • Lives in Denver
  • Active lifestyle
  • Interested in health
  • Leans into natural ingredients

.

Get to Know Maria

As a loyal customer, Maria’s extensive purchase history favors products with natural ingredients and healthy attributes.

Maria proactively manages her family’s health and well-being, purchasing things like adult & kids vitamins and probiotics. When she tries something new, like sleep aids, she sticks to leading brands.

Maria’s favorite incentives, like BOGO or featuring health-based product benefits, motivate her to purchase especially when she is researching products.

Given her regular purchasing habits, Maria might also be interested in other products that prioritize health and picks up new items when she sees in-store signage or coupons.

Meet Evie

Evie is always on trend. That eyeliner fad? She’s already done it. New lipstick launch? It’s in her basket already. Evie represents one of millions of data profiles in wag’s BPC advanced audience solutions.

  • Female, 28
  • Lives in Chicago
  • Heavy mobile app usage
  • Prefers 30-min delivery

.

Get to know Evie

Because of Evie’s repeated purchases of new beauty products, she and others like her are a part of wag’s “off the shelf” early adopter beauty audience.

Her purchases of trending beauty items, like eyebrow gels and shiny bold lipsticks, indicate she’d purchase others in the future, too.

She’s highly likely to respond to “new” products – and will have them delivered. She’s most likely to engage with videos and convert via mobile.

Her interest in beauty could extend to things such as infused water, hair & nail vitamins, travel-sized items and more.

Meet Samuel

Sam seems to have a new status: caregiver. Given his most recent purchases of certain items – like a cane and heart-healthy vitamins, it’s safe to assume he has more than just himself in his household.

  • Male, 55
  • Lives in Philadelphia​
  • Typically shops online
  • 30-min delivery fan

.

Get to Know Samuel

Sam has a new status: caregiver. Given his purchases, wag can determine what needs he or a loved one may have.

Sam may have an elderly adult in his household given the type and frequency of purchases like incontinence aids, nutritional drinks, and a blood-pressure monitor.

Sam leans into the ease and convenience Walgreens offers, especially 30-min. delivery. He also tends to respond to dollars-off offers he sees on favorite sites.

Sam’s always been a regular purchaser of on-the-go snacks and drinks. He also recently stepped up purchasing of paper products.
